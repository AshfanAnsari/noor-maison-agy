Questions

How do I enquire about a piece?

Tap Enquire on any piece and leave your name, phone number and a short message. Saba reads every enquiry and replies to you herself.

Are prices listed on the website?

No — every piece is priced on enquiry. Ask about any piece through the enquiry form and Saba will confirm its price and availability personally.

How do I pay?

No payment is taken on this website. Orders are confirmed personally with Saba after your enquiry.

What happens to my details?

Only your name, phone number and enquiry message are collected, so Saba can respond to your request. Ask at any time and your details will be deleted.
